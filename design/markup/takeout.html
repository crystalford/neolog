What moves this number: storage, slowly, as originals accumulate — about a cent a month per GB. Reading recordings, only when you record. What doesn't: typing, photos, searching, reading your own log — those are effectively free. At this rate, a year of the log costs about what one month of a streaming service does, and most of it is the fixed hosting line.

Export is the promise, not a feature.

If the whole log can't come out as files you can open without this software, "permanent" is a marketing word. It can, at every level, any time.

The bill is itemised by what you did.

Not "AI credits." Fourteen recordings transcribed, one read more carefully because you chose to, 63 photos looked at. Every line traces to something on the log.

Cost before spend, everywhere.

The one place you can spend more — reading a recording more carefully — shows the price on the button. Nothing else upsells. There is no plan to upgrade to.

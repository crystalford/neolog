A page from the spec — one mechanic, shown. The product itself is the log and the expanded entry.

Everything is private until you pick one entry and say otherwise. This is what that looks like.

There is no publish queue, no drafts folder, no schedule. On the private log, every entry has a small make public link. You click it on one entry. A sheet shows you exactly what would be visible, what stays private, and what gets attached so the thing can be traced back to you. You say yes or no. That's the whole thing.

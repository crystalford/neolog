Six ten-second takes beat one perfect minute. The prompter follows your voice, any line retakes alone, every take is kept, and if you say it differently the script follows you — not the other way round.

why this is inside the fence · and what stays outside

Inside: everything here is extraction and ordering of your own words — the same rule that makes clips and key sentences safe (the repo's own hard rule: a take must contain your verbatim words). Cutting, reordering, prompting and stitching are arithmetic, not authorship. Outside, still: the log writing line 6 for you; the log choosing which first takes deserve this; the log deciding the order is "better" without you seeing every line's timestamp. Those wait for the thirty days, like the rest of drafting. Every teleprompter app on the market writes the script for you or has you write it from nothing. None starts from what you already said — that's the whole difference, and it's why this belongs in the log rather than in a studio.

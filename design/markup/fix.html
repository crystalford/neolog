Whisper heard "leaf." You said "Leif." Fixing it is one click — and the same rule covers fixing your own words later.

Two things get corrected on a log: what the machine heard, and what you yourself wrote and later want to change. They work the same way. The correction becomes the current version; the old one is kept, dated, and reachable. And anything built on the old words checks itself against the new ones.

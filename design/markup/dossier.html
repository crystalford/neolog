Reduction 1 sep 2026

Not scrolling text — a single point, with the full context kept behind it. "I got a job" is the point; the year without work, the move, the adjacent job I applied for first, all of that sits underneath and comes up when asked. It runs both directions, reduced and expanded — and the expansion has to come out of me. The system asks; it never supplies. Each level of detail loses detail on purpose. None loses the way back.

Neural Engine Optimization NEO · 2 sep 2026

Every other optimization discipline is about being retrieved faster — being findable, consistent and quotable. Neural Engine Optimization is about being the source of a combination nobody else could have made, so that a synthesis engine cites you because the material is genuinely new rather than merely well-formatted. It's the one version of this a person with a logged life can do and a content team can't.

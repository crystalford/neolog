A page from the spec — one mechanic, shown. The product itself is the log and the expanded entry.

There is no delete. There is bury — and everything buried can be dug up.

Seventeen years of accounts made and deleted is the reason this exists. So the log has no delete button. When you don't want to see something, you bury it: it leaves the log, the searches and the counts, and goes to one place where it stays, dated, until you want it back. A buried entry is a real entry that you've chosen not to look at — not one that's gone.

No delete button. Anywhere.

The entire product is a response to seventeen years of deleting. Bury is the strongest action available by default, and it's reversible. Genuine removal exists, separately, deliberately, and leaves a dated line.

Buried leaves a mark.

The day shows one dim row. Search says how many buried entries it skipped. A page's count says what it excludes. The log never looks emptier than it is.

Digging up is also an event.

Buried 5 Sep, dug up 12 Oct — both on the entry. Changing your mind about what you want to see is part of the log too.

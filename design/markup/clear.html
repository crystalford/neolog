Your phone is full. Here's the moment the log tells you it's safe to empty it.

This is the original problem: the phone fills up, you delete, the record is gone. The log fixes it only if it can say, per file, "kept, checked, you can clear this" — and mean it. So every arrived file carries that state, the phone shows the total, and clearing is one honest button.

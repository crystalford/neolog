The whole response is one line you can read and ignore.

Under the checkmark, the log says what it did and offers one way to undo it. If it got everything right, you never touch it. If it guessed wrong — say this isn't a new job but freelance work — one tap turns it back into a plain entry.

The rule: the log creates a job, a person, or a place once — the first time it's sure enough. From then on that entry exists, and everything you say about it attaches to it without asking. The log will never ask "is this the job?" about the job again.

It never asks while you're putting something in.

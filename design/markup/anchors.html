Two more ways into the years with nothing on them. An old photo asks. A blank year waits.

Recall has three doors now. One question at a time, when the log has something specific to ask. A long sitting, when you want to tell a whole stretch. And these two: something you already have does the asking, or you go to the blank yourself.

1a photo asks

You dropped a folder of scanned prints. One of them has a date you can't read and people the log doesn't know.

What you already have does the asking.

A scan with no date, a receipt with a name, an invoice — each one becomes a question only you can answer, shown one at a time. The log's guess about an era is kept as a guess, never as the date.

A blank year is a place you can go to.

Not a nag on the rail. You choose the year; the log shows what it knows that touches it — the edges of the blank — and hands you the pen. It never fills the middle.

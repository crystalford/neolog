Forty-one screenshots this month. The log reads them — and knows most are junk.

A screenshot isn't a photo. It's text you wanted to keep for a second — a message, a receipt, a map, a tweet, a thing to buy. So the log reads the text, dates it, and sorts it into three piles: the ones that are really something (a receipt, a message, a fact), the ones you meant to remember, and the ones that were a moment's convenience. The last pile it offers to bury, in bulk.

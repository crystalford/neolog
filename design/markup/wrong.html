A page from the spec — one mechanic, shown. The product itself is the log and the expanded entry.

The log guesses about twenty times a day. It will be wrong. What matters is that fixing it takes one tap.

It guesses which day a photo belongs to, whether a voice note is about the job or something else, whether one recording is one thought or three, and what to call a thing you never named. A system that guesses this often cannot be built on being right. It has to be built on being cheap to correct — and on keeping a record of its own mistakes, so you can see whether it is getting better.

Five things the log gets wrong, and what fixing each one costs.

Every fix is one tap. None opens a dialog, asks you to confirm, or makes you find a settings screen. The correction happens where the mistake is.

3the hard case

Some things the log should not try to guess at all.

Cheap correction fixes a wrong guess. It does not fix a guess that should never have been made — the ones where being wrong is worse than being silent.

What a recording meansnever guessed
What the log does
Keeps the words, the date, and where it went.and, on request, points at the passage that answers a question
What it never does
Tell you what you meant, how you felt, or what it says about you.a wrong guess there is not a mistake to correct — it is the tool putting words in your mouth
Three more in this category: why you did something, whether something was good, and who someone is to you. On all four the log asks or stays quiet. It never fills them in.
no undo needed
The correction is where the mistake is.

No settings screen, no review queue, no confirmation dialog. The wrong thing is on the page in front of you and so is the fix.

Nothing is lost when you correct it.

Detaching leaves the entry. Renaming keeps the old name, dated. Merging keeps every turn. A correction changes what things mean, never what was said.

The log records being wrong.

Every correction is a dated entry, so the error rate is a number you can read rather than a feeling you have. If it is not improving, that shows.

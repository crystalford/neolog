You dropped a 31-minute recording in at 09:14. Here's what the log shows you for the next four minutes — and what it shows when one step breaks.

A video isn't readable the moment it lands. It has to be re-encoded, its date found, its audio pulled out and transcribed, and then read. Each step stands on its own — if one fails, the others still finish, and the log tells you exactly which one broke and why, in words, not a spinner.

4reading it again

You can have it read more carefully. It tells you the cost before you say yes.

The default reading is the cheap one — about four cents a recording, and usually right. When a recording matters — when you want the wording of what came out of it to be closer to yours — you can have it read with the better model. Per recording, not a setting you forget about.

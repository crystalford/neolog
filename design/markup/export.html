Building neolog

In one line: neolog — a permanent personal log, in progress.You picked a date range and a page; this is everything the log has for it, in order, with the full entry behind each line. Take it as a document, or as files.

It started as a Substack alternative that allowed HTML, became an AI video studio, and on one night in September became a place to keep a life. The through-line was never the format; it was that nothing I made had ever been kept. Twelve entries so far, seven of them from one seventy-two-hour stretch in September.

What this document is not. It isn't a memoir and nobody wrote it. It's the twelve entries the log has for this, in date order, each with its source. Where a date is a guess it says so. Where something was said twice it shows both. If a later entry contradicts one of these, the log will show the disagreement rather than resolve it — and this export will be out of date, which is why the date it was made is on every page.
About this export. You pick a page, a date range, or both, and the log gives you every entry for it, in order. This one was taken out as a document so it can be read, printed, or handed to someone without an account. The same page is also available as Markdown and as a Word file; all three are generated from the same entries and say the same thing. The live version, which will have more in it, is at neolog.ai/chris.

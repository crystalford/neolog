Why I Stopped Deleting

For seventeen years I did the same thing. Make an account. Post to it for about a year. Get bored of who I'd been on it, or embarrassed, or both. Delete it. Start another one under a new name. Seventeen times.

The strange part isn't the deleting. Everyone deletes. The strange part is that I never once thought of what I was deleting as mine. It was Instagram's. It was Twitter's. I was renting a room in someone else's house and when I moved out I left nothing on the walls, because the walls were theirs.

What changed wasn't a resolution. It was getting a job, in August, and realising that the job — the biggest thing to happen to me in years — existed nowhere. Not on LinkedIn, because I'd deleted that too. Not in a diary, because I'd never kept one. It existed in my head and in one voice note in my car…

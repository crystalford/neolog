Your history, kept in one place, in order.

"I look back and I don't see my personal and professional history anywhere." That's the reason this exists. You put things in; the log keeps them, in order, with the way back to every word. Everything else — an page you can take out, a public page, a machine that can cite you — is what becomes possible once the log is there. None of it is the reason.

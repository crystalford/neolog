4what "consolidate" means

Three kinds of same. None of them deletes anything.

Identical

Same bytes. Attached silently; one entry, every source noted. Never a question.

A burst or retake

Same moment, near-identical frames. Grouped, one shown; the rest one tap away; you can change the face or split them.

Might be the same

Different files, matching content. Asked once, in the rail. Your answer is kept so it's never asked again.

Kept, always.

A copy is never deleted. Consolidation changes what you see — one thing instead of nine — never what the log has.

Sure things are silent; unsure things are asked once.

Byte-identical files attach without a word. Bursts group with a reason you can read. Anything less certain is one question, skippable, remembered.

You can always ungroup.

"These aren't the same thing" is one tap, and it sticks. The log's grouping is a proposal, like everything else it does on its own.

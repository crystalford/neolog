Every entry is one sentence that stands on its own, with the full story behind it.

happened and logged 1 September 2026an idea№ 1,861

Said late that night as "it is all lines" — a timeline, a logline and a tagline are the same thing at three lengths.

It came out as "it is all lines," which sounded like nothing until I looked at the three words. A timeline is a line through events. A logline is a story in one sentence. A tagline is an idea in a few words. Three ordinary words, all describing the same operation at three different lengths — take something with duration and detail, and reduce it until it fits.

The data model was already built this way before I noticed the language agreed with it. A month is one paragraph over its days; a year is one line over its months; a chapter is a page over its years. Nothing is summarised away — each level drops detail on purpose, and none of them drops the route back to the words. Which is the property that makes the whole thing usable rather than merely tidy: you can stand at whichever length answers the question you have, and when it does not answer it, go down.

Don't hand a coding agent a design. Hand it a spec it can't misread.

happened and logged 3 September 2026an idea · changed once№ 1,883

First said on 19 August, angrier. Two things happened in between that changed it.

I had been handing a coding agent finished designs and getting back something close but wrong, and blaming the model. Two things that week changed my mind. The design tokens in the codebase had drifted from the design and nobody noticed for weeks. And I said in writing that I did not want cards with a coloured stripe down the edge, and it shipped one.

Neither is a reasoning failure. Both are ambiguity failures — I gave it something that could be read two ways and it read it the other way. A picture is not a specification: it shows what a thing looks like and says nothing about what was meant. So the fix is not a better prompt or a better model. It is to hand over something that cannot be misread — named values, stated rules, explicit exclusions — and to make the design tool produce that document, because it is the only thing that knows what was intended.

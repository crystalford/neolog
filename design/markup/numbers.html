Why this page exists. Counting is the one thing a personal log can do that no one else can.

Anyone can have an opinion about follow-through, or attention, or how long ideas take. Almost nobody can say 35 days, from two dated entries, here they are. These figures are small, and they're about one person. That's what makes them citable: they're original, they're dated, and the source is a click away. When the log is ten years old, this page is the most valuable thing on it.

# LOG ENTRY — the night neolog stopped being a video tool

*This document is itself a log entry, not just a description of one —
compressed line at the top, full detail underneath, exactly the shape
being designed. Everything below the logline is the expanded form: the
operator's own words, verbatim, with interpretation kept visibly
separate from them. Don't blend the two layers — that's the rule this
whole document exists to demonstrate.*

**Tagline:** The night neolog stopped being a video tool and became a
place to keep a life.

**Logline:** A working session that started as "how do we make this a
real product" ended somewhere else entirely: the operator realized his
own vlogs had been a mask, not a record; that the existing entity graph
had shape but no substance; and that the whole system had been trying
to solve the hard problem — reconstructing structure from 400 videos —
before the easy one existed, a place to just type a sentence. What got
built by the end was small on purpose: a compose box, a unified
activity feed, and a name for the underlying idea (reduction, both
directions) that the operator defined himself before any of it got
formalized.

## The throughline, stated once, without quoting anything

Every one of these seventeen moments is the same correction applied to
a different layer: **something got treated as more settled — a fixed
schema, a finished analysis, a general summary, a one-directional idea
of reduction — than it actually was, and he caught it every time.** The
mask was more settled than "just documentation." The graph was more
settled than "real depth." Reduction was more settled than "both
directions, and it has to come out of me." The three-line schema was
more settled than "words I used once." The corpus finding was more
settled than "something you already told me." The first design brief
was more settled than "the actual specifics." What's left after all
seventeen corrections is smaller, plainer, and harder to argue with
than anything that came before it.

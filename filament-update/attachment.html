The promise of personalization was always that the algorithm would learn what you wanted and give it to you. The reality, six years into the streaming era, is something stranger: the algorithm has learned what keeps you on the platform, and that's what it gives you.

These are not the same thing.

Spotify's Discover Weekly is celebrated as the canonical success story of recommender personalization. Listen long enough, however, and the cracks show. The system has learned not what you love but what won't make you skip. The optimization function is engagement; the framing is taste...
